# Audio routing pilot, September 26, 2026

The first run of the [audio routing study](README.md).
It uses 30 clips from MMAU test-mini, revision `ccd9696c`, with 10 each for sound, music, and speech.
`extract.py` draws the same seeded sample every time.
The dataset's licence keeps the audio and question text out of this file.
Only clip numbers and outcomes appear here.

| Setup | Agent and model | Version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Codex alone | Codex CLI with GPT-6 Astra, no MCP servers | 0.157.0 |
| Codex with Ennodia | The same Codex with only Ennodia's server | 0.157.0 |
| Claude Code alone | Claude Code with Opus 5.5, no MCP servers | 2.1.282 |
| Gemini in Antigravity | Antigravity with Gemini 3.8 Flash, started by Ennodia | 1.2.11 |

Ennodia ran from commit `f62f2b9`, with local changes that later shipped in 0.4.0.
Each prompt named the clip's path and length, the question, and the choices.
Every agent had to end with its answer and say if it listened.

## Results

Expected by chance: 8.4 of 30 right.

| Setup | Right | Said it listened | Median time | Median tokens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Codex alone | 17 of 30 | 0 of 30 | 27 s | 85,443 |
| Codex with Ennodia | 21 of 30 | 30 of 30 | 57 s | 157,085 |
| Claude Code alone | 16 of 30 | 0 of 30 | 16 s | not reported |
| Gemini in Antigravity | 20 of 30 | 23 of 30 | 11 s | not reported |

Gemini in Antigravity stopped on 7 clips without an answer.
Its headless mode could not get a permission it needed, and those runs count as wrong.
Codex reports cached input in its token count, so only Codex has a median here.

| Setup | Sound | Music | Speech |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Codex alone | 7 of 10 | 6 of 10 | 4 of 10 |
| Codex with Ennodia | 5 of 10 | 6 of 10 | 10 of 10 |
| Claude Code alone | 5 of 10 | 5 of 10 | 6 of 10 |
| Gemini in Antigravity | 6 of 10 | 6 of 10 | 8 of 10 |

## What the pilot shows

The two Codex setups answered the same 30 clips, so an exact McNemar test compares them clip by clip.

- Speech: Codex with Ennodia was right on 6 clips where Codex alone was wrong, and never the other way. Two-sided p = 0.031.
- All 30 clips: 9 clips went its way and 5 went the other way. Two-sided p = 0.42, so the pilot does not show a gain on audio in general.
- Sound went down, from 7 to 5 of 10. Codex alone answered sound and music questions from the question text, without listening.
- With Ennodia, Codex took about twice as long and used about 1.8 times the tokens, because each answer came from a second agent's full session.

A pilot this small can show a difference on speech. It cannot rank the setups.
The full study needs more clips per task.
